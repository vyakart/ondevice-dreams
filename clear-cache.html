🧹 CLEAR CACHE & SERVICE WORKER

STEP 1: Click the button below to unregister the service worker
STEP 2: Clear browser cache
  • Chrome/Edge: Press Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows)
  • Safari: Press Cmd+Option+E
  • Firefox: Press Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows)
Select "Cached images and files" and click Clear
STEP 3: IMPORTANT! Go to this URL:

http://localhost:8888

NOT file:///...

NOT http://127.0.0.1:8000

MUST BE http://localhost:8888

STEP 4: Verify CORS headers (open DevTools Console and run):

await fetch('/').then(r => r.headers.get('Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy'))

Should return: "same-origin"
